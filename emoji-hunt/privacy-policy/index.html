Emoji Hunt Privacy Policy

Effective date: 18 November 2026

Emoji Hunt is provided by NorthStarPixel. This policy explains how the game handles information.

Information stored on your device

The game saves level progress, best score, and your selected age range (under 13 or 13 or older) in local app storage. The game does not require an account and does not ask for your name, birthday, or contact details. NorthStarPixel does not receive the saved progress or age-range choice. Clearing app storage or uninstalling the game removes this locally saved information.

Advertising, consent, and information handled by Google

Emoji Hunt uses Google Mobile Ads to request and display banner ads and Google’s User Messaging Platform (UMP) to manage consent choices where required. The Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and share your device’s IP address, app interactions, diagnostics, and device or other identifiers (which may include an advertising ID). IP addresses may be used to estimate approximate location. Google may use this information for advertising, analytics, fraud prevention, security, and compliance. Google encrypts SDK data in transit.

The app applies child-directed ad treatment when the under-13 age range is selected and gates ad requests on the consent status provided by UMP. NorthStarPixel does not receive the age-range choice. Google handles information under its own policies; see Google’s Privacy Policy and Google Mobile Ads data disclosure.

When Google requires a privacy-options form for your region, you can open it from Settings → Privacy choices in the game.

Children

Emoji Hunt is intended for a broad audience, including children. The game provides an age-range choice so ad requests can use the corresponding child or teen treatment. The ad service may still handle information as described above, subject to applicable consent and ad settings.

Retention and deletion

Game progress, best score, and the age-range choice remain in local app storage until you clear that storage or uninstall the game. Google handles advertising information under its own retention practices. The game does not provide an account or a server-side profile to delete.

Changes to this policy

If the game’s data practices change, we will update this policy.
