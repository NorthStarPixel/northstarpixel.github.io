Staff Loop Privacy Policy

Effective date: 21 September 2026

Staff Loop: Interview Prep is provided by NorthStarPixel. This policy explains how the current version of the app handles information.

Information we collect

Staff Loop does not collect personal information. The app does not require an account, sign-in, or registration. It does not use advertising, analytics, tracking, or third-party data collection services.

Information stored on your device

Your saved questions and learning progress are stored only on your device so the app can remember them between sessions. This information is not sent to NorthStarPixel or any third party. Clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the app removes this locally stored information.

Permissions and network access

The current version does not request Android runtime permissions and does not send the question bank, your progress, or your saved items over the internet.

Sharing and selling data

Because Staff Loop does not collect personal information, we do not sell, rent, or share personal data.

Children's privacy

Staff Loop is an interview-preparation app and is not directed to children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

If a future version changes how the app handles data, we will update this page and the in-app privacy policy before or when that version is released.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact NorthStarPixel using the developer contact email shown on the Staff Loop Google Play listing.
